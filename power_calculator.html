ℹ️ Assumptions & Limitations
  • Two-sided test at α = 0.05 (fixed; z = 1.96).
  • Continuous, normally distributed outcome, standardized to SD = 1.
  • This tool computes MDEs given sample sizes — it does not compute required N given a target MDE.
  • Does not account for attrition, non-response, or multiple treatment arms.
  • Clustering formula assumes equal cluster sizes.
  • Stata commands are for verification; sampsi uses a slightly different algorithm and may yield marginally different results.
