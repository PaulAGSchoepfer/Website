01 About

About me

I am a Junior Researcher at Utrecht University School of Economics and am finishing my PhD in Development Economics at Wageningen University & Research. I am currently on the job market.

My work combines randomized controlled trials and quasi-experimental methods with hands-on fieldwork in The Gambia, Kenya, Nigeria, Malawi, and Bulgaria. Since 2022 I have designed and run field experiments together with ministries of education, NGOs, and survey teams, and presented the results to policymakers, including the Gambian Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education and the Vice President of The Gambia.

I grew up in Graz, Austria, and studied economics at the University of Vienna before moving to the Netherlands for an MSc in Economic Policy at Utrecht University. Before academia I worked at the Austrian Federal Chancellery during the EU Council presidency and at the Austrian Financial Market Authority. Alongside my research I teach master's courses that bring students into direct contact with partners in East Africa, and I was guest editor of a special issue on international community-engaged learning.

02 Research

Research

My dissertation, Enhancing Life Outcomes: The Interplay of Holistic Skills, Preschool Education, and Technology of Instruction, consists of three papers on educational interventions in low- and middle-income countries.

Work in progress

  1. 2026
    Does Extra Exam Practice Raise Test Scores? Experimental Evidence from Kenya and Nigeria
    with Guthrie Gray-Lobe, Ronak Jain, Michael Kremer, Joost de Laat, and Becky Scurlock

    Two cluster-randomized trials on frequent testing in 245 schools with more than 3,700 students. We use administrative test-score records and national leaving-exam data to estimate whether additional exam practice improves learning.

    Draft, available on request
  2. 2026
    Strengthening Student Outcomes Through Active Learning Methods. Experimental Evidence from The Gambia
    with Joost de Laat, Thomas van Huizen, Hamidou Jawara, and Tomohisa Miyamoto

    A randomized evaluation of a national competency-based curriculum reform and teacher training in 90 primary schools with more than 2,100 students, implemented together with the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education.

    Draft, available on request
  3. 2026
    Parental Beliefs and Home Investment. Evidence from Bulgaria
    with Joost de Laat, Esther Gehrke, and Thomas van Huizen

    A survey of 1,000 parent-child pairs across 121 communities with a vignette-based belief elicitation that separates beliefs about how skills are produced from beliefs about the returns to skills.

    Draft, available on request

Ongoing field projects

I lead design-based research pilots on garden-based education and foundational literacy and numeracy in Kenya and Malawi as principal investigator, in partnership with Edukans and local universities. I am an external reviewer for the Network of Impact Evaluation Researchers in Africa (NIERA) and co-author research proposals for funders including J-PAL and the Finnish National Agency for Education.

03 Teaching

Teaching

My teaching connects economics to real problems and real partners. Students in my courses work with organizations in the Netherlands and in rural Kenya, and their projects feed back into the research and practice of those partners.
