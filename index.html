01 About

About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Development Economics at Wageningen University & Research, based in Amsterdam. My research focuses on enhancing life outcomes through education — examining skills development, access to education, and pedagogical innovations in low-income contexts.

Alongside my PhD, I work as a Junior Researcher at Utrecht University School of Economics, where I design and conduct randomised controlled trials (RCTs) and quasi-experimental evaluations, with a particular focus on education in Sub-Saharan Africa. I lecture at master's level and am an active member of the International Community Engaged Learning (ICEL) team, co-developing the UEngage platform and contributing to a special journal issue on community-engaged learning.

I am committed to bridging rigorous academic research with real-world policy impact, and enjoy collaborating with academics, policymakers, and practitioners across disciplines.
